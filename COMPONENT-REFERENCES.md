# My Car Atlas — component reference inventory

This project is inspired by **my own 2024 BMW X5 40i M-Sport (G05 LCI)**. The sources below guide its educational reconstruction.

Vehicle-specific details were checked against the owner-supplied screenshot and bimmer.work vehicle/options pages on 9 September 2026. These are decoder-reported specifications, not a factory build-sheet certification. The VIN and its record URL are not bundled. Paint is Alpine White III (300), represented with an approximate non-metallic white material. The record confirms type 21EU, B58B30M2, European left-hand drive, 740 M wheels (1TC), runflat tyres (258), M Sport brakes (2NH), Sport automatic (2TB), Adaptive M suspension (2VF), M Sport/aerodynamics (337/715), Shadowline trim/rails (760/3MC) and adaptive LED headlights (552). Silverstone Sensafin (KPA9) and the listed production date/location appear in About; there is no cabin reconstruction. No exact tyre sizes, damper behaviour or caliper dimensions are inferred.

The exterior adapts BMW’s CC BY 4.0 G05 model for the **G05 LCI M Sport**. Mechanical parts remain illustrative; none of these references establishes VIN-specific CAD. See [body provenance](dist/models/README.md).

The engine targets **B58N / B58B30M2**, identified for the LCI 40i in [BMW service bulletin B00 03 23](https://bmwtechinfo.bmwgroup.com/sib_attachments/B00%2003%2023h.pdf). It supersedes the former B58B30M1-family photo. The European left-hand-drive xDrive40i catalogue type is **21EU**. The 50e and M60i are separate powertrains.

The owner-supplied X5 50e and X6 M cutaway renders (12 September 2026) guide **packaging proportions only**: engine height beneath the bonnet, position between the front wheels, a compact gearbox behind the engine, and a longer, slender rear propeller shaft. The model retains its B58B30M2 and GA8HP60X geometry. The engine, gearbox and transfer case are calibrated within the existing G05 body; shared connection planes keep the housings, propeller shafts and engine-mount seats joined after rescaling. The 50e underfloor battery and high-voltage cabling, its plug-in transmission hardware, and the X6 M V8 are excluded. These perspective images establish no measured 40i dimensions or factory clearances.

Checked 8 September 2026: the requested [Leebmann engine collection](https://www.leebmann24.com/de/en/collection/bmw-spare-parts-engine-10032) returned HTTP 403 / Cloudflare in both direct downloading and a normal browser. No images were obtained from Leebmann. The accessible [KOED engine group](https://www.koedbmw.com/en-dk/catalog/group/21EU/11?steering=L&transmission=A) and [mounting group](https://www.koedbmw.com/en-dk/catalog/group/21EU/22?steering=L&transmission=A) supplied **all 26 BMW engine drawings and both suspension drawings listed for that type**. Retail tools and consumable advertisements were excluded. One matching donor rear photograph supplements them. The engine has 28 browsable images; the gearbox suspension drawing appears under Transmission.

[The image manifest](dist/references/engine/manifest.json) records every original image URL, file, SHA-256 checksum, applicability and rights. These are BMW catalogue illustrations, with the original copyright marks retained, plus a seller photograph. No redistribution license has been established. Catalogue diagrams can show shared parts and later service replacements; this collection does not establish VIN-specific part numbers or measured geometry. The engine surfaces are modelled from these references; the cutaway is a static educational reconstruction.

Five paired spoke groups replace the former broad straight spokes. Both images are bundled under `dist/references/wheels/`, with ownership retained by their respective sources. Tyre geometry and forward rotation are unchanged.

The owner’s additional [hybrid layout image](https://www.automotivepowertraintechnologyinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/6a00d8341c4fbe53ef02b7516ffc46200b-1024x605.jpg) is bundled as a secondary transmission reference. Its large underfloor battery and high-voltage cabling indicate a plug-in hybrid, so it guides only broad longitudinal placement. BMW specifies an engine-compartment 48V battery for the 40i; the illustrated underfloor packs are not added to this model.
